        How it works

        1. Allow location once. Your iPhone asks twice: first “While Using the App,” then “Always.” Say yes both times, and the app can note the country even on days you never open it.
        2. Each day gets a stamp. The app records the country your iPhone is in and stamps the day in that country’s ink. There are no blank days: it assumes you stayed where you were until a new reading shows otherwise. If a day is wrong, tap it and fix it.
        3. The counts add up. See your days in each country for this year, any past year, or the last 365 days. When your accountant asks, make a PDF or a spreadsheet in a couple of taps.

        Your days stay on your iPhone

        • Only the date and the country are saved. Not your street, and not a trail of map points.
        • No account, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
        • There is no Where You Were server. Your days are never sent to me or to anyone else.
        • Free. Nothing is locked.

        Questions, answered

        Is it really free?

        Yes. No price, no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases and no account to make.

        Which countries does it work in?

        All of them. Wherever your iPhone can find its location and look up the country, the day is counted. Each new country gets its own ink, in the order you first visit.

        What if my phone doesn’t get a reading one day?

        The day isn’t left blank. The app assumes you stayed where you were until a new reading shows otherwise. If that’s wrong, tap the day and choose the right country.

        Can I share my days with my accountant?

        Yes. Make a PDF and a spreadsheet file (CSV) for one year or for all years, and send them however you like.
